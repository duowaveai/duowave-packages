# The text of an episode

Concept first, then words: a line exists only if it moves the situation.

## First episode (40–75 s)

| Piece | Seconds | What |
|---|---|---|
| Set-up | 0–8 | One sentence in the second person: place, job or incident, the stake, the rule |
| Alone | 8–12 | Why no help comes |
| Event | 12–20 | The world breaks the rule right in front of the hero |
| Clues | 20–35 | Two or three details that do not add up; each is its own shot |
| Pressure | 35–45 | A second voice hurries you or the threat comes closer |
| Fork | last 8–10 | The price of both options in words, then the question |

A new event every 5–10 seconds. Up to 0.3 s between lines.

## The fork

1. Each option has a price. A free or plainly fatal option is no choice.
2. The hero does not know the right answer.
3. Earlier episodes have not answered this question yet.
4. The price is said plainly, in the voice and on the cards.
5. The stake is about the threat, not money. "Whom to trust" beats "where to go".

## The narrator

- Says "you", in short sentences. No hero's name.
- Never names what is seen in the frame; says what the picture cannot say.
- Never asks to rewatch and never says "in the next episode".
- Characters' lines in quotes “…”.

## Both branches and the rewind

Each episode after the first shows both branches of the previous fork: the wrong one to a bad end («THE END»), one
line "Let's rewind", then the right one, a reveal, a new threat, a new fork. Which option is wrong is decided by the
law of the world, never by chance. The bad end is different every time.

## Words that are not there

Nowhere — voice, subtitles, screen: "die", "death", "kill", anything about suicide. Say "won't last till morning",
"can't leave", "not long left".
